I am a Herchel Smith scholar (one-year graduate student) at Harvard University in the Statistical Reinforcement Learning group, hosted by Susan Murphy. I completed my PhD in statistics at the University of Cambridge in June 2023, supervised by Rajen Shah. My research interest lies at the intersection of statistics and machine learning.
